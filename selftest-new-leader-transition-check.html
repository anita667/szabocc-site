New Leader Transition Check

In my experience, the move from top performer to people leader is where things get hard. What made you successful as a specialist stops being enough, and there is rarely time to work out what replaces it.

This short check helps you see where you stand. Twelve statements, about three minutes. Rate each one by how true it has been for you over the last month.

Who it is for: leaders in their first formal leadership role, or in their first year or two after a promotion.
