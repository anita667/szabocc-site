Change Readiness: one level below

Change is decided at the top. It succeeds or fails one level below.

This check looks at the leaders who will carry your next change: the managers one level below the decision. Think of one specific group, for example the leaders reporting to you, or the leaders in the area you are responsible for. Twelve statements, about three minutes. Rate each one by how true it is for that group today.

Who it is for: senior leaders and the people who decide about leadership development in an organisation facing, or already in, a significant change.

Think of the leaders one level below the decision. Statements 1 to 11 are about them; statement 12 is about you.
