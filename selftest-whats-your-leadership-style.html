What's your leadership style?

Every leader has a style they reach for first, especially under pressure. Seeing it clearly is the first step to using it on purpose.

This check builds on the six leadership styles described by Daniel Goleman in “Leadership That Gets Results”, Harvard Business Review, March–April 2000: coercive, authoritative, affiliative, democratic, pacesetting and coaching. Twelve statements, about four minutes. Rate each one by how well it describes what you usually do when you lead a team, not what you would like to do.
